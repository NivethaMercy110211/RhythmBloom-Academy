Our Faculty

Meet Our Instructors

Passionate performing artists who are equally dedicated educators — each bringing professional expertise, authentic technique, and genuine love for teaching to every class.

Priya Sharma
Lead Classical Dance Instructor
Bharatanatyam Kuchipudi
Classical Dance · 14 Years Experience

Priya Sharma

Bharatanatyam & Classical Indian Dance

"Dance is not just technique — it is a language of the soul. My mission is to help every child discover that language within themselves."

A disciple of the Kalakshetra tradition with a Master's in Performing Arts from the University of Madras, Priya joined RhythmBloom in 2016. She believes that classical dance training offers children far more than movement — it develops patience, focus, cultural pride, and a sense of artistic identity that lasts a lifetime. Her teaching methodology blends traditional guru-shishya pedagogy with modern child-friendly communication.

National Sangeet Natak Akademi scholarship recipient, 2011
Performed at the Khajuraho Dance Festival and Chennai Margazhi Season
Choreographed 6 annual Arangetrams for advanced students
Certified examiner for Visharada and Kovid examinations
BharatanatyamKuchipudiAbhinayaNattuvangamCarnatic Rhythms

Western Dance · 10 Years Experience

Meera Kapoor

Contemporary, Jazz & Lyrical Dance

"I want every child to walk out of class feeling stronger, more expressive, and more joyful than when they walked in. That is the real measure of a great class."

Trained at the Shiamak Davar Institute of Performing Arts in Mumbai and further at the Broadway Dance Center in New York, Meera brings an international perspective to her classes. She specialises in fusing technical precision with creative freedom — building young dancers who can perform with both discipline and heart. Her intermediate and advanced classes regularly produce competition-winning choreographies.

Featured choreographer at the Jio MAMI Mumbai Film Festival 2019
Trained under the Batsheva Dance Company masterclass series
Her intermediate batch won Best Group at Kolkata Youth Dance Championship 2025

Rahul Singh

Hip-Hop, Breaking & Urban Dance

"Hip-hop gave me a voice, a community, and a purpose. I want every kid who walks into my class to feel that same power — regardless of where they come from."

A former national-level breaking champion with 8 years of teaching experience, Rahul's classes are a perfect balance of high energy and genuine craft. He takes great care to design age-appropriate content that respects hip-hop's rich cultural roots while creating a safe, inclusive, and wildly fun environment for children aged 7 to 16.

National Breaking Championship — gold medalist, 2018 & 2020
Workshop facilitator at Red Bull BC One Camp India, 2022
Choreographed the RhythmBloom hip-hop showcase featured at Kolkata Youth Fest

Drama & Theatre · 11 Years Experience

Ananya Bose

Drama, Acting & Theatrical Direction

"Every child is already a storyteller. My job is simply to give them the tools, the courage, and the stage to share their stories with the world."

A graduate of the National School of Drama, New Delhi, Ananya has over a decade of experience in both professional theatre and children's performing arts education. She uses play-based learning, improvisation, and devising techniques to build not just young actors — but confident communicators and creative thinkers ready for any stage life offers.

National School of Drama graduate, Class of 2013
Directed 8 full-scale children's theatrical productions at RhythmBloom
Guest faculty at Jadavpur University Department of Comparative Literature

Voice Training Performance
Vocals & Voice · 9 Years Experience

Supriya Das

Vocals, Voice Training & Singing Performance

"The voice is the most personal instrument a person will ever have. Teaching a child to use it with confidence and joy is one of the most meaningful things I can do."

A classically trained vocalist with a background in Hindustani music and contemporary performance, Supriya creates a deeply supportive space for young singers at every level. Her curriculum blends traditional riyaz practices with modern vocal pedagogy — covering breath support, resonance, pitch accuracy, and performance confidence in equal measure.

Diploma in Hindustani Vocal Music, Sangit Mahavidyalaya, Kolkata
Performed at ITC Sangeet Research Academy's annual showcase
Three of her students have won state-level youth music competitions
