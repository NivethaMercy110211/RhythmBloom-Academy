Our Story

About RhythmBloom Academy

Founded on the belief that every child deserves to discover the transformative power of performing arts — we have been nurturing young artists in Kolkata since 2014.

Our Founding

Where RhythmBloom Began

Founded in 2014 by dancer and educator Priya Sharma, RhythmBloom began with one clear purpose: to help every child discover their artistic voice in a welcoming, confidence-building environment.

From a single studio with 12 students, the academy has grown into a five-discipline community of more than 500 young artists while keeping each child's experience at the heart of every class.

How We Teach

Our Teaching Philosophy

Six core principles guide every class, rehearsal, and recital at RhythmBloom Academy — ensuring technical excellence develops alongside joy, confidence, and authentic creative expression.

Child-Centred Safety First Joyful Excellence

Child-Centred Learning

We meet every child exactly where they are. Progress is individual — never comparative. Each student's unique journey is celebrated and respected.

Safety & Wellbeing First

Physical safety and emotional wellbeing are non-negotiable. Our studios are judgment-free zones where children feel safe to try, fail, and try again.

Excellence Without Pressure

We hold high standards while keeping joy at the centre. Technical excellence and fun are not opposites — they reinforce each other when taught well.

Authentic Artistic Identity

We do not produce carbon copies. Our curriculum encourages every student to develop their personal artistic voice alongside technical discipline.

Community & Belonging

RhythmBloom is a community. Students, families, and faculty grow together — celebrating milestones, supporting each other, and building lifelong friendships.

Performance as Education

The stage is the classroom. Recitals and performances are learning experiences — not tests — that build real confidence, resilience, and pride.

Our Commitment

A Safe & Supportive Learning Environment

Every class at RhythmBloom Academy is designed to help children feel physically safe, emotionally supported, and confident enough to learn and express themselves at their own natural pace.

  • Verified, experienced teaching faculty with background checks
  • Small batches (max 12 students) with dedicated personal attention
  • An inclusive, body-positive curriculum celebrating every student's growth
Where We Create

Our Studios

Purpose-built spaces designed for young performers — with professional sprung floors, mirrors, sound systems, and warm, inspiring atmospheres.

Studio 1 — Classical & Western Sanctuary
Studio 2 — Contemporary & Hip-Hop Arena
Studio 3 — Drama & Vocal Black Box

Music Room

Acoustically treated vocal training room with piano, audio recording setup, and individual practice pods for the Vocals program.

Faculty Lounge

Private workspace for instructors to plan curriculum, review student progress, and prepare choreography between sessions.

Parent Lounge

A comfortable waiting area with Wi-Fi, seating, and a viewing window so parents can observe classes whenever they wish.
