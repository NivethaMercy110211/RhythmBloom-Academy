Classical Dance

Classical Dance

Bharatanatyam & Classical Indian Traditions

Rooted in centuries of tradition, our Classical Dance program offers authentic Bharatanatyam training and introduces students to the rich heritage of Indian classical arts. Students learn fundamental adavus, hastas (hand gestures), facial expressions (abhinaya), and rhythmic footwork (nattuvangam) in a disciplined yet joyful environment.

Ages 5–16 Beginner to Advanced 60 min/class Sat & Sun batches Max 12 students
Authentic Kalakshetra-tradition curriculum taught by experienced gurus
Annual Arangetram preparation for advanced students
Performance opportunities at cultural events and academy recitals
Theory of classical music rhythms (tala) integrated into curriculum

Western Dance

Contemporary, Jazz & Lyrical Styles

Our Western Dance program fuses technical rigour with artistic freedom. Students explore the full range of contemporary movement, jazz technique, and lyrical expression — developing strength, flexibility, musicality, and a deeply personal performance voice.

Ages 6–16 All Levels 60 min/class Mon/Wed/Fri batches
Foundation in ballet-based technique for proper body alignment
Original group choreography created for each recital season
Improvisation workshops to develop unique artistic expression
Cross-training with drama for enhanced stage presence

Hip-Hop Dance

Rhythm, Breaking, Popping & Freestyle

Energy, attitude, and artistry — our age-appropriate Hip-Hop program introduces children to the rich culture and technical vocabulary of urban dance. From foundational grooves and toprock to freezes and choreography, students develop rhythmic precision and boundless confidence.

Ages 7–16 Beginner & Intermediate 60 min/class Tue & Thu batches
Age-appropriate curriculum — no adult hip-hop content
Freestyle sessions encourage personal creative expression
Battle-format workshops build confidence and sportsmanship
Taught by a national-level competition champion instructor

Drama & Theatre

Acting, Storytelling & Stage Confidence

Our Drama program goes beyond scripts and costumes — it builds the very foundation of confident communication. Students explore character building, emotional expression, improvisation, voice projection, and ensemble work through games, exercises, and full theatrical productions.

Ages 6–16 All Levels 75 min/class Sat & weekday batches
Annual theatrical production — full script, costumes & set design
Voice projection, diction, and public speaking skills embedded
Proven to improve academic confidence and social skills
Collaborative writing workshops for older students

Vocals & Voice

Voice Training, Breath Control & Performance

Every child has a voice — our Vocals program helps them find it, develop it, and share it with the world. From proper breathing technique and pitch training to performance anxiety management and stage presence, our certified vocal coaches create a nurturing space for every young singer to flourish.

Ages 5–16 All Levels 45 min/class Flexible scheduling
Individual and group vocal training sessions available
Breathing exercises, posture, and pitch accuracy training
Performance at inter-academy showcases and community events
Music theory integrated into the curriculum for older students
