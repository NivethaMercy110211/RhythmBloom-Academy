Frequently Asked Questions

We welcome children from age 5 onwards. Our youngest students join the Beginner batches for Classical Dance or Vocals. For Hip-Hop we recommend age 7+. There is no upper age limit for our programs.
Yes! We offer a complimentary trial class for new students in any program. Simply contact us to schedule a trial and your child can experience the class before you commit to enrollment.
Fees vary by program and number of classes per week. Monthly fees range from ₹2,500 to ₹4,500 depending on the program. There is a one-time annual registration fee of ₹1,000. Full fee details are available in the Parent Dashboard after registration.
Yes, mid-term enrollment is possible for most programs subject to seat availability. Your child will be assessed by the instructor to place them in the appropriate level. Contact us to check current seat availability.
Yes — costume fees are charged once per term for students participating in the showcase. Fees vary by program (typically ₹1,500 to ₹3,500) and are announced 8 weeks before each showcase with full details.
Students are welcome to attend a make-up class in a batch of the same level within the same term, subject to availability. We track attendance through the Parent Dashboard so families always have a clear picture of their child's progress.
